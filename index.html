自己紹介Origin story

I'm a backend engineer who likes the unglamorous half of the stack — the queues, the retries, the reconciliation job that has to be right at 2am.

I started on a Fortune 500 airline's data platform at Hexaware, moving five million events a day through Lambda and Kinesis. Then I co-founded Digitalyft and built algorithmic trading infrastructure where a 500ms delay is a real cost, not a metric on a dashboard. Today I'm at GrayQuest, where I led the Golang migration of our backend services and own the payment rails the business runs on.

The pattern across all three: take a system that works but strains, understand exactly where it strains, and rebuild that part properly. Usually that means concurrency done carefully, events instead of polling, and enough observability to prove it worked.
